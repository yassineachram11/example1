In short: delivery is free everywhere in Lebanon, arrives within 1–5 working days, and you pay the courier in cash when it reaches you.

Where we deliver

We deliver anywhere in Lebanon. We do not ship outside the country at the moment.

What it costs

Nothing. Delivery is free on every order, whatever you order and wherever you are. There is no minimum.

When it arrives

Orders are handed to the courier within one business day of being placed. Delivery then takes 1–5 working days depending on where you are — Beirut and the surrounding area is usually the fast end of that range.

These are estimates from our courier, not guarantees. Weather, road conditions and public holidays can add a day.

Paying on delivery

Orders are paid in cash when the courier hands over your pillow. Nothing is charged before then.

Please have the exact amount ready if you can — couriers do not always carry change. The amount due is the order total shown at checkout, with no delivery fee added on top.

If you are not there

The courier will call you before delivering, so please make sure the phone number on your order is one you actually answer. A wrong or unanswered number is the single most common reason an order is delayed.

If nobody is available, the courier will try again. After repeated failed attempts the order comes back to us and we will contact you to arrange another delivery.

Tracking your order

You will get an email when your order is on its way. If you want to know where it has reached, email shoppiloco@gmail.com with your order number and we will check with the courier.

Something wrong with your delivery

If your order arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, see our Refund Policy — we cover it.

Questions

Email shoppiloco@gmail.com. We answer every day of the week.
