In short: Pilo pillows can be returned within 14 days of delivery, but only if the packaging is still sealed and unopened. Once a pillow has been opened we cannot take it back. If your order arrives damaged, faulty or wrong, that is on us and we will replace it or refund you in full.

Returning an unopened pillow

You have 14 days from the day your order is delivered to ask for a return. To qualify, the pillow must be:

  • unopened, unused, and still in its original sealed packaging
  • undamaged, with no tears, writing or tape on the packaging

If the seal is broken, we cannot accept the return.

Why only sealed pillows

A pillow sits against your face for hours every night. Once its packaging is opened we have no way to verify it has not been slept on, and we will not resell an item like that to somebody else. This is the same reason mattresses, underwear and other close-contact products are sold on these terms. It is not a way of avoiding returns — it is the only condition under which we can promise the next customer an untouched product.

We would rather you were certain before you order. If you are unsure whether the Pilo 1.0 suits how you sleep, message us first and we will tell you honestly.

How to start a return

Email shoppiloco@gmail.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and the reason for the return. We will reply with where to send it.

Please do not send anything back before you hear from us — returns that arrive unannounced are difficult to match to an order.

For a change of mind, you arrange and pay for the return delivery. If the item was damaged, faulty or not what you ordered, we cover it.

Refunds

Because orders are paid in cash on delivery, refunds are made by cash or bank transfer rather than back to a card. We will agree the method with you when you contact us.

Once your return reaches us and we have checked the seal, we will refund you within 7 business days. Delivery was free, so the full amount you paid is refunded.

Damaged, faulty or wrong items

Check your order when it arrives. If the box is damaged, the pillow is faulty, or you have been sent the wrong pack, email shoppiloco@gmail.com within 48 hours of delivery with your order number and photos.

We will replace the item or refund you in full, whichever you prefer, and we cover all delivery costs both ways. The sealed-packaging condition above does not apply in these cases — open it, check it, and tell us.

Cancelling or refusing an order

You can cancel free of charge any time before your order leaves us. Email us as soon as you can and we will stop it if it has not yet been collected by the courier.

You can also simply decline the delivery at the door and pay nothing. We would appreciate a message if you know in advance that you no longer want the order, so we are not sending a courier across the country for nothing.
