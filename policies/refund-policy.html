We want you to be happy with your purchase. If something isn't right, you have 30 days from the day your order arrives to request a return.

Starting a return

Email us at shoppiloco@gmail.com with your order number and let us know what you'd like to return. We'll confirm the return and send you instructions for getting the item back to us.

Please contact us before sending anything back. Items returned without an approved return request can't be accepted.

Return shipping

Return shipping is free. Once your return is approved, we cover the cost of sending the item back to us.

Condition of returned items

To be eligible for a return, the item must be unused and in the same condition you received it, with all original packaging and accessories included. Please include your order number or proof of purchase.

Damaged, defective or incorrect items

Please check your order as soon as it arrives. If the item is damaged, defective, or not what you ordered, contact us right away at shoppiloco@gmail.com and we will put it right with a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer. Where the law gives you a longer period for faulty goods, that period applies instead of the 30 days above.

Items we can't accept

Gift cards can't be returned or refunded.

Exchanges

The fastest way to get a different item is to return the one you have and place a new order once your return is approved.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we'll email you to confirm whether it was approved. Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 10 business days.

Depending on your bank or card issuer, it can take a few more days for the money to appear on your statement. If more than 15 business days have passed since we approved your refund and you still haven't received it, email us at shoppiloco@gmail.com.

Questions

Email shoppiloco@gmail.com and we'll help.
